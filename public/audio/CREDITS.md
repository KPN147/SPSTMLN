## Adaptations

Full recordings retained. Adaptations: high-pass at 45 Hz, low-pass at 6.5 kHz, loudness normalization with a target of −22 LUFS / true peak −4 dBTP / LRA 7, fade-in 2.5 seconds and fade-out 3 seconds, stereo Ogg Vorbis encoding. No tempo or pitch changes.

License and authorship were checked on the individual asset pages, not inferred from a collection title. Source retrieval: 2026-11-08. Source-page snapshots, source checksums and processing report are retained in `.build/music-sources/`. Runtime playback uses local files, not streaming from OpenGameArt.

The eight interaction sound effects are separate original synthesized effects. `scripts/generate-audio.mjs` regenerates only those effects and preserves these background tracks.
